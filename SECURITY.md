# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover any security issue or vulnerability on the
Bhakar Cement & Stone Supplier website, please report it privately.

## Responsible Disclosure

Please follow these guidelines:

1. Do not publicly disclose security vulnerabilities.
2. Do not access or modify other people's personal information.
3. Do not damage the website or its data.
4. Share details of the issue privately through WhatsApp.

## Response

We will review reported security issues and take appropriate
action as soon as possible.

Thank you for helping us keep our website secure.
